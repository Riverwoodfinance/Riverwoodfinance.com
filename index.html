Data quality

Comparability starts after extraction.

Two KIDs can use the same label and still measure different things. Riverwood tracks basis, period, currency, methodology, peer eligibility and document history before data is used analytically.

Capture and extraction become cheaper over time. The durable value sits in entity resolution, normalization, historical versions, peer logic, availability and decision workflows.

Quality state

A visible state follows each datapoint through the pipeline.

10 Jan2028 · ESAP

Finding KIDs becomes easier. Knowing what is comparable does not.

From 10 January 2028, PRIIP key information documents made public must also be submitted for access through the European Single Access Point. Riverwood is built above the collection layer: normalization, history, peer eligibility, availability and decision infrastructure.
