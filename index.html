A KID is at most three pages. The work sits behind them.

Three pages. A much bigger obligation.

Before a covered product is made available to retail investors, its manufacturer must prepare and publish a KID. The obligation reaches beyond the document to its calculations, its review and the procedures behind it.

Supervisors look at more than the finished PDF. They ask how the figures were calculated, who is responsible, and how the document is kept up to date when facts change.

Corrective orders

Revised documents, calculations or procedures.

Financial penalties

Sanctions under the applicable national framework.

Public disclosure

The responsible party and the infringement may be named.

Marketing restrictions

Marketing may be suspended or prohibited in relevant cases.

Legal basis: PRIIPs Regulation, Articles 22-29. Measures depend on the circumstances; there is no standard penalty for every KID error.

The next review doesn’t start from zero.

PRIIPs review rules require an assessment at least every 12 months and whenever relevant changes arise. A review and a new document are different events.

A control result has value, even when the figures stay the same.

Review guidance: CSSF, 28 May 2024 and the Danish FSA. Rules: PRIIPs RTS.

  1. Establish the basis

    Validate the supported profile, inputs and initial control evidence.

  2. Watch for the next trigger

    Track relevant input and rule changes, review dates and agreed thresholds.

  3. Reassess, and recalculate when needed

    Reuse the method and record what was checked, what changed and what needs action.

  4. Keep the versions connected

    Link any new release to its earlier basis and set the next control point.

Before we start

Clear scope is part of a credible production process.

Is Riverwood available for full production today?

Riverwood is in development. Conversations about scoped reviews, paid pilots and future production are welcome. A pilot or limited review is not the same as a fully launched platform; each engagement depends on agreed scope, method fit and readiness.

Does every investment fund need a KID?

No. The requirement depends on the product and its investors and distribution, not simply a manager’s registration. Our initial scope also depends on supported methods and the necessary inputs. Read the Danish FSA guidance.

Can a review confirm the underlying calculation?

Not from a PDF alone. A document review can assess the visible information and its consistency. Checking a method or reproducing a calculation requires the relevant source data and assumptions. The agreed evidence level and any untested areas are stated explicitly.

Who remains responsible for the KID?

The product manufacturer keeps its regulatory responsibility and approves and publishes the KID. Responsibilities for inputs, publication and corrections are agreed explicitly. A review is a control, not a legal opinion or a guarantee against supervisory findings.

Will confidential inputs be used for shared peer data?

Not automatically. Private client inputs and calculations are kept apart from common market and peer data. Any shared use or model-training use needs its own authorised basis and purpose.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

Your organisation, product type, distribution country, approximate number of KIDs and the next KID or review deadline. A link to a public KID helps define the task. Please do not send confidential inputs before a data-sharing arrangement is agreed.
