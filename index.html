What we build

Three products, one data model.

Funds without market data are the hard case. There is no price series, so the risk measure has to be established by proxy, the scenarios have to be calibrated by judgment, and the costs have to be traced through the structures beneath the fund. That work does not get cheaper by running it faster — it gets safer by writing down what was decided and why.

Workbench Structured intake, deterministic calculation and explicit points where a judgment is required rather than assumed. Every delivery is archived with its inputs, its engine version and its approver, so it can be recreated exactly as it stood. White label for law firms, compliance houses and administrators
Fund Data Cost, risk and scenario fields drawn from published documents and normalised for currency, reporting period, fee basis and calculation method. Every field carries a source reference, a capture timestamp and a quality state. Nordic coverage first, then wider European
Product Governance Cost benchmarks against a defined peer group, detection of disclosure anomalies such as a stress scenario that outperforms the unfavourable one, method deviation against comparable funds, and monitoring of what changed between versions. Not investment advice. No fund rankings, no return forecasts.

Quality state

  • Q0Document captured and hashed
  • Q1Fields extracted with confidence score
  • Q2Checked against the document
  • Q3Normalised for basis and period
  • Q4Cleared for a named peer group
  • Q5Underlying method note held

Capture and extraction are becoming commodities. Everything from Q3 upward requires knowing how the number was produced — which is the part that cannot be automated away.
